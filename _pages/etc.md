## Trivia

I am a _[sansei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sansei){:target="_blank"}_. My second name, Koji (Kōji), is a reference to my grandfather's name. In Japanese, my name is written 宮本 エンヒッケ 弘二.

My mother tongue is [Brazilian Portuguese](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lh9jvtLeTLQ){:target="_blank"}.

I am a fan of [Studio Ghibli films](./porco.jpeg), and of Joe Hisaishi's [soundtracks](https://youtu.be/faf98cNY8A8){:target="_blank"}. My favourite film is [Princess Mononoke](https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20220713-princess-mononoke-the-masterpiece-that-flummoxed-the-us){:target="_blank"}.

My [MR collaboration distance](./collaboration-distance) to Claude Shannon is 4.
