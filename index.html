Henrique K. Miyamoto

Researcher | Information theory

I am a Brazilian researcher. My research interests lie around information theory and its applications (MSC2020 94A and 94B).

I prepared my Ph.D. degree at the Laboratory of Signals and Systems (L2S), which is jointly operated by CNRS, CentraleSupélec and Université Paris-Saclay in Gif-sur-Yvette, France. I was advised by Prof. Sheng Yang.

Prior to that, I received the M.Sc. degree in applied mathematics from Unicamp, Brazil, the Diplôme d'Ingénieur (major: applied mathematics) from CentraleSupélec, France, and the B.Sc. degree in electrical engineering from Unicamp, Brazil.
